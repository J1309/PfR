How to Add Your Next Exam Track

Whenever you are ready to prepare for a new exam, follow these simple steps:

1
Create your data file: Add your question or cheatsheet array inside js/data/yourExamData.js.
2
Link it in index.html: Include <script src="js/data/yourExamData.js"></script> before js/app.js.
3
Tell the agent: Simply state which exam you're preparing for and share your syllabus or PDFs to generate full drill decks instantly.
