The 4-Day Zero-to-Qualifying Playbook

Tailored specifically for an MCA Graduate with 0 previous commerce background.

Day 1: Oct 11

Master Pension Sector & Management

Goal: Secure 20 guaranteed marks in Paper 2.

  • Read the Pension Sector Cheat Sheet (PFRDA Act, NPS Tier 1 vs 2, APY age & slabs).
  • Complete all 35 questions in the Pension & PFRDA Drill.
  • Complete all 30 questions in the Management Drill (Maslow, Herzberg, McGregor).
  • Target study time: 4 to 5 hours.
Day 2: Oct 12

Financial System & High-Yield Paper 1

Goal: Solidify financial terminology and build high-speed accuracy in Paper 1.

  • Study Money Market instruments (T-Bills, CP, CD, Call Money, Repo Rate).
  • Run through the Finance & Regulators Drill (30 Questions).
  • Run through the Quant, Reasoning & English Drill (Leverage your MCA analytical edge).
  • Target study time: 4 to 5 hours.
Day 3: Oct 13

Companies Act, Costing & Full IBPS Mock

Goal: Learn low-hanging sections of Companies Act and practice exam pacing.

  • Review Companies Act highlights (OPC, Director limits, Board meetings).
  • Take a timed Paper 2 IBPS Simulation Test (40 mins).
  • Analyze penalties! If your accuracy is below 75%, stop guessing on 50-50 questions.
Day 4: Oct 14

The Final Polish & Revision Deck

Goal: Retain facts and ensure psychological calmness.

  • Review all questions inside your Starred Revision Deck.
  • Re-read the APY and NPS quick reference table.
  • Pack your Admit Card, Original Photo ID (Aadhaar/PAN), photocopy, and 2 passport photos.
  • Sleep by 10:30 PM. A well-rested brain gives you a +10 marks advantage in Paper 1 reasoning.

🛡️ Official IBPS Exam Day Rules & Interactive Packing Checklist

Strict IBPS & PFRDA regulatory compliance requirements for 15 October 2026. Check off each item before leaving home!

📋 1. Mandatory Documents (Check Each as You Pack)

⚠️ Crucial Warning: The photocopy must be of the exact same ID you present in original. If you carry original PAN card, the photocopy MUST be of PAN card, not Aadhaar. Name on ID must match Admit Card character-by-character!

👕 2. Clothing & Dress Code Protocol (IBPS Biometric Hall Rules)

✅ Allowed & Recommended
  • Half-sleeved light shirts / t-shirts (no long folded sleeves).
  • Simple trousers, cotton pants, or jeans without excessive zippers.
  • Slippers or sandals with thin soles (easy frisking).
  • Prescription spectacles (checked during frisking).
🚫 Strictly Prohibited
  • Shoes, boots, high heels or thick-soled footwear.
  • Clothes with large buttons, badges, brooches, or metallic decorations.
  • Smartwatches, digital/analog wristwatches (time is displayed on screen).
  • Belts with big metallic buckles, jewelry, chains, rings, or earrings.
  • Wallets, calculators, bluetooth devices, or earphones.

⏱️ 3. Reporting Time & Biometrics Procedure

Arrival Window

Reach the exam venue 45 minutes before the printed reporting time. Test center gates close strictly 15 minutes before the exam begins; no entry is permitted thereafter under any circumstance.

Biometric Capture

Thumb impression and digital photo capture take place before seat allocation. Ensure your fingers are clean and free from ink, chemical residue, or mehendi/henna.
